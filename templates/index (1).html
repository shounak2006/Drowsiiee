Core Capabilities
◎

Eye Aspect Ratio

Soukupová & Čech (2016) EAR formula applied across six MediaPipe facial landmarks per eye. Threshold-based closure detection at 0.25.

⧖

Fatigue Scoring

Rolling fatigue score (0–100) that rises with eye closure and excessive blinking, and decays when the driver is alert. States: SAFE / WARNING / CRITICAL.

◈

Face Mesh Tracking

MediaPipe FaceMesh with 468 landmarks. Precise eye contour rendering, blink detection, and head pose resilience at 30 fps on CPU.

▲

Instant Alerts

Audio alarm triggered on 2-second eye closure or critical fatigue threshold. Visual HUD overlay on the live video stream.

◱

Live Dashboard

Real-time metric cards, rolling Chart.js fatigue graph, closure duration, and blink rate — all updating at 500ms intervals.

◬

Session Analytics

Per-hour fatigue aggregation, trend detection, and downloadable JSON logs for post-session review or academic reporting.
